Frequently asked

The questions we get most

My unit died and I have been told to replace it. Is that right?

Sometimes, often not. Capacitors, contactors, clogged drains and dirty coils account for a lot of midsummer failures. Get the fault diagnosed and the repair priced — a free second opinion costs you nothing.

Why does sizing matter? Surely bigger is better.

No. An oversized system cools quickly, shuts off, and never runs long enough to remove humidity, so the house ends up cold and clammy. Sizing comes from a load calculation for your building.

Will a new system cut my bill in half?

We will not promise a figure. Efficiency ratings are measured under test conditions, and your actual consumption depends on the building, the ductwork, your usage and the weather.

One room never gets cold. Do I need a bigger unit?

Usually not. Test the ducts first — leaking attic ductwork delivers cooling into the attic rather than the room, and that is frequently the whole explanation.

Do you need a permit to swap my system?

Yes, where the local authority requires one, and we pull it. A replacement done without a permit can cause problems at resale and with insurance.

What happens to the old refrigerant?

It is recovered, not vented. That is federal law under Section 608, and our technicians are certified for it.
