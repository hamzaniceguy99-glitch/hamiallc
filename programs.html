Program 02 · Calculated, not matched

Replacement & sizing

Replacing a three-ton unit with a three-ton unit is not sizing, it is copying a decision somebody made decades ago. An oversized system cools fast, short-cycles and leaves the house cold and clammy, because it never runs long enough to pull humidity out. We calculate, and permits are pulled.

  • 1 to 2 days
  • Homes and businesses
  • Quoted in writing
  • Permit approval

Program 04 · Where the cooling goes

Ducts & airflow

A correctly sized system fed by leaking attic ducts delivers a fraction of its capacity into the house and the rest into the attic. Before anyone sells you a bigger unit for the room that never cools, the ducts are worth testing — the answer is frequently there.
